# Hearth

**The home. Climate, devices, and sensors.**

*In development*

## Climate

**Every room in one place**\
Power, temperature, mode, fan speed, and airflow for each room’s air conditioner.

**Comfort settings**\
Sleep, quiet, turbo, and more. If a unit can’t apply one, Hearth tells you.

**Constant**\
Keep a room’s temperature setting in place while its air conditioner is on.

**Temperature history**\
Each room’s temperature over the last 24 hours.

## Routines

**Schedules**\
Create a recurring morning, bedtime, or away schedule for any combination of rooms.

**Holds**\
Change a room in Hearth and its schedule waits an hour. Hold it longer, up to a day, or resume early.

**Off timers**\
Drift off. Hearth turns the AC off later.

**Scenes**\
Set your rooms just right, then save a scene.

**Device routines**\
Set it once. Feel it every day. Schedule a supported device setting.

## Devices

**Lights, air, and sensors**\
Hearth shows the controls each device offers: brightness, color, color temperature, humidity targets, and scenes.

**Sensor history**\
Readings build a 24-hour history. Offline readings are labeled and left out.

**Matter**\
Add a Matter accessory and keep the controller it already has.

## Smart rules

**A home that pays attention**\
When a reading rises above or falls below a line you set, a device responds.

**Steady**\
A rule waits before responding again and resets only after the reading crosses back.

**Fresh readings only**\
A stale or offline reading never sets off a rule.

## Intelligence

**Suggestions first**\
Hearth looks at your rooms and suggests changes, each with the readings behind it. Approve one, or set it aside.

**Your kind of comfortable**\
Set a comfort range for each room, and tell Hearth when you usually wake, leave, get back, and sleep.

**The bigger picture**\
The weather just outside and your energy bills inform suggestions.

## Household

**Under one roof**\
Invite people, choose their access, and keep everyone’s space their own.

**The right amount of access**\
Give each person control of a room or a device, view only, or no access at all.

**A guided tour**\
Take a quick look at your rooms, devices, and routines.

## Every change, checked

**Checked**\
Hearth reads a device before every change and checks it after.

**Unconfirmed**\
If a change can’t be confirmed, Hearth says so. It never sends the change again on its own.

**Activity**\
Every change and connection event, in one log.

## Runs at home

Hearth runs on a Mac at home. Your history, routines, and accounts live there.
